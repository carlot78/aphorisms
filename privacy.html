Privacy policy

Aphorisms — effective 13 October 2026

Aphorisms shows one thought a day from authors you choose. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. This page explains, plainly, what stays on your device and what leaves it.

What stays on your device

Your themes and followed authors, your ratings, streak and laurels, saved thoughts, history, your own passages, and downloaded quotes are stored only in your browser / app storage on this device. They are never uploaded. Removing the app, clearing its data, or using Reset the app deletes them.

These services receive your device's IP address as part of any internet request, under their own privacy policies. We do not sell or share data with anyone else.

Text you share into the app

Passages you send to Aphorisms from other apps (for example a Kindle highlight) and Kindle clippings you import stay on your device. They are never sent to our database.

Children

Aphorisms is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Your choices

You can withdraw any rating by tapping the same face again, turn off translation and reminders at any time, and delete everything on the device with Reset the app or by uninstalling. Because ratings are anonymous we cannot link them to you; to have the anonymous ratings from your device removed, use Reset the app after withdrawing them, or contact us with the device id shown below.
