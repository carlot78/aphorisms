Privacy policy

Aphorisms — effective 25 October 2026

Aphorisms shows one thought a day from authors you choose. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. This page explains, plainly, what stays on your device and what leaves it.

What stays on your device

Your themes and followed authors, your ratings, streak and laurels, saved thoughts, history, your own passages, and downloaded quotes are stored only in your browser / app storage on this device. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app, clearing its data, or using Delete my data removes them (see Deleting your data).

Text you share into the app

Passages you send to Aphorisms from other apps (for example a Kindle highlight) and Kindle clippings you import stay on your device. They are never sent to our database.

Children

Aphorisms is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data from Aphorisms

Aphorisms (developer: carlot78) has no accounts. Everything is tied to an anonymous id that the app creates on your device, so you can delete it all yourself, at any time:

  1. Open Aphorisms and tap the menu (☰, top right).
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my data, then confirm.

This immediately and permanently deletes:

  • on our server: all your anonymous ratings (🙁 / 😐 / 🙂), your daily-reminder subscription (push address, hour, time zone, themes, authors, language) and any problem reports you sent;
  • on your device: your themes and followed authors, ratings, streak and laurels, saved thoughts, history, your own passages, translations and downloaded quotes.

Nothing is kept afterwards: there are no backups of individual records, and no data is retained for any additional period. Totals that were computed from your ratings before deletion are recalculated without them.

Other ways: withdrawing a single rating (tap the same face again) deletes it from the server; turning the daily reminder off deletes the subscription; uninstalling the app deletes the data on the device. If you can no longer open the app (for example, you already uninstalled it) and want the server copies removed, open an issue at github.com/carlot78/aphorisms/issues with your anonymous device id if you have it — we delete the data within 30 days. Reminder subscriptions that stop working are also deleted automatically.
